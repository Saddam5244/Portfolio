Smart Farming Platform

AI Weather Forecast & Advisory Engine

Farmers often face severe crop losses due to unpredicted downpours, frost, and high humidity fungal outbreaks. WeatherGPT translates raw meteorological metrics into clear, actionable advice in plain language.

  • Natural Language Queries: Farmers can ask directly about watering timing, seed sowing suitability, and fertilizer windows.
  • Hyper-Local Weather Radar: Live humidity, precipitation intensity, and temperature tracking tailored for agricultural zones.
  • Automated Agronomy Safeguards: Real-time alerts to postpone nitrogen spraying during heavy rain risks to prevent runoff.
  • Built with Python & APIs: Powered by OpenAI GPT model integration and high-precision weather APIs.
